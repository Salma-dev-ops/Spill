# Spill


Spill is an offline-first journalism and community platform built for Africa, with a focus on helping communities surface, share, and discuss local stories that matter. The platform is designed to support storytelling, civic awareness, and community engagement in environments where internet access may be unreliable or limited.

At its core, Spill gives communities a way to document issues, discover relevant narratives, and participate in public conversations that might otherwise remain invisible. By organizing stories around communities, topics, and local concerns, Spill helps turn lived experiences into accessible, shareable, and actionable knowledge.

## Why Spill?

Many communities, especially in underserved regions, lack a reliable digital space to tell their stories, raise awareness, or hold institutions accountable. Traditional media channels are often centralized, slow to respond, and disconnected from the realities of everyday communities.

Spill addresses this gap by creating a simple, community-centered storytelling platform that is designed to work even in low-connectivity environments. It supports offline-first access, keeps conversations local and relevant, and helps amplify voices that are often overlooked.

## What the platform does

- Showcases community stories and local reporting
- Organizes content around communities and topics
- Encourages civic awareness and local engagement
- Supports offline-first access for low-connectivity settings
- Creates a clean and accessible frontend experience for discovery and browsing
- Radio self hosting and real time AI language transcription.

## Current state of the project

The repository currently contains the foundation of the product: a modern React + TypeScript frontend, a landing page, Radio and a community feed experience. These features demonstrate the platform's value proposition and provide a strong base for future functionality.

## Run locally

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the URL Vite prints, then visit `/` for the landing page or `/communities` for the community feed.
